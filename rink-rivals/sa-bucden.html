Latest

Nov 15 · Injury reportMayfield has a lower-leg injury.

NFL insider Mike Garafolo reports Baker Mayfield hurt his lower leg against the Bears in Week 9 and has been in and out of a walking boot. It is not an injured-reserve injury and is expected to be short-term, but he may miss the Nov 22 game at Detroit. Jalon Daniels would start if he is out.

Nov 16 · Bye weekWeek 10 off, then Detroit.

Tampa Bay (3-6) rests this week and visits the 7-2 Lions on Sunday, Nov 22 at 1:00 PM ET.

Nov 10 · Trade deadlineBucs stand pat.

The deadline passed at 4 PM Tuesday with no trade. Reports describe Tampa Bay as neither a buyer nor a seller, and note that GM Jason Licht rarely makes in-season trades.

Nov 8 · Week 9Bears 27, Bucs 24.
