🔥 Rival Nights

Survive in the forest. By day, chop trees 🌲, pick berries 🍓 and mine rocks 🪨. Keep the campfire burning. At night the shadow critters come out of the dark. They hate light, so build torches, zap them, and never let the fire go out!
