🏆 The Rink Rivals Classic

Step on stage against four other bodybuilders. Pump up backstage, hit the seven mandatory poses when the head judge calls them, then win the crowd in the pose-down. Five judges rank everyone — lowest total wins.
Show

Poses

Backstage: tap Space as fast as you can to get a pump. Prejudging: when a pose is called, press its key (1–7) or click it — fast is better. Then hold Space to flex and keep the flex meter in the green: too soft looks flat, too hard and you shake. Pose-down: 25 seconds to hit any poses you like (1–8) — every pose you lock in the green wins the crowd, and variety scores more. Your physique comes from your strength in the gym.
