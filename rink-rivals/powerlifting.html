🏋️ Powerlifting Meet

A full meet: three attempts each at the squat, bench press and deadlift. Pick your weights, wait for the head referee's commands, and get at least two white lights for a good lift. Best squat + best bench + best deadlift = your total. Highest total wins.
Meet
—
Squat: Space to unrack → wait for "SQUAT!" → hold Space to sit down, let go once you're below parallel (green) → hit Space in the zone to drive up → wait for "RACK!" and press Space. Bench: unrack → hold to lower the bar to your chest, let go → keep still until "PRESS!" → hit the zone → wait for "RACK!". Deadlift: hit the zone to pull → lock out → wait for "DOWN!". Moving before a command is a red light. Heavier attempts shrink the zone — go too heavy and the bar won't move.
