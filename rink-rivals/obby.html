🧗 Neon Obby

Run, jump and climb your way through 12 stages of floating platforms high above the lava: moving blocks, spinning bars, tiles that vanish under your feet, jump pads, conveyor belts and a final tower. Checkpoints save your progress. Race the clock — or race your friends online.
Mode
Move WASD / arrows (the way the camera faces) · Jump Space · Turn the camera drag the screen or Q E · R back to the last checkpoint. Red and orange glowing blocks are lava — don't touch them. Green pads launch you high, blue arrows are conveyor belts, flashing tiles drop away a moment after you land.
