⚔️ Rival Deflect

A glowing ball hunts one player at a time. When it's coming for you (it turns your color), hit Space, click or tap DEFLECT right before it reaches you. It flies at someone else, a little faster every time. Deflect too early and you're stuck for a moment. Last one standing wins!

Move with WASD / arrows (on phones, drag the left side).
