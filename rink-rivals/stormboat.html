🌊 Storm Boat

A storm has caught your trawler out at sea. Get her home through mountains of water: climb every wave bow-first, ease off at the crest so you don't launch off the top, and never let a wave catch you side-on. Pump out the water you take over the bow, watch your fuel, and look out for the rogue waves — twice the size of the rest.
Storm
Throttle W / S · Steer A D · Pump hold E (or Space) · C camera. How to survive: point into the waves (a little off straight-on is best) · power up the face · back off at the top · don't race down the back or you'll bury the bow. If the roll meter fills you're about to capsize — turn back into the sea.
