💪 Arm Wrestling

Standing pro table, best of three. Explode off the "GO", mash to drive their hand down, and read their technique — counter a top roll or a hook before it costs you the pin. Your gym strength is your arm.
Start: on "READY… GO!" press Space right on GO (too early is a foul). Pull: tap Space fast (or click) to push — it burns energy, so ease off to recover. Counters: when they go for a move, press the counter fast: A vs TOP ROLL · D vs HOOK · W vs PRESS. Pin their hand to the pad to win the round.
