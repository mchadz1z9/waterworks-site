🚀 Launch Pad

Build a rocket, light the engines and fly it to space yourself. Air gets thinner as you climb, engines change with altitude, every kilo of fuel counts, and the only way to stay up is to go sideways fast enough to keep missing the ground — that's an orbit.

Throttle W/S (or Shift/Ctrl) · Z full · X cut · Pitch A D · Space launch / next stage · M map · C or mouse wheel zoom · G auto gravity turn · T hold prograde · P parachute · , . time warp (in space, engines off) · Esc abort.
How to reach orbit: go straight up to about 1 km, then lean slowly east (D) so you're at 45° by ~12 km and nearly flat by ~45 km. Watch Max Q — leaning hard in thick air tears the rocket apart. Burn until your apoapsis (highest point) is above 80 km, cut the engine, coast up to it, then burn flat (prograde) until the periapsis also clears 70 km.
