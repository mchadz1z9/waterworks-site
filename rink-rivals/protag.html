🏃 Pro Tag

Professional chase tag on a 12 × 12 metre court packed with vault boxes, a centre platform and walls. Every chase lasts 20 seconds: the chaser has to tag the evader — if the evader survives, they score a point and stay on to evade again. Get tagged and the roles swap. Most points after 12 chases wins; a tie goes to a sudden-death chase.
Opponent
Run WASD / arrows · Jump / vault Space (onto boxes and the platform) · Dive Shift — as chaser it's a lunging tag with longer reach, as evader it's a quick juke sideways. Both leave you a moment to recover, so time them. Leaving the court while evading counts as a tag. Use the walls to break line of sight and run circles around the boxes.
