🔥 Workout of the day

Walk with WASD or the arrows (hold Shift to run), or click the floor to walk there. Walk up to a station and press E (or click it) to use it. Lifting: each rep, hit Space or click when the needle is in the green zone — dead centre in the gold is a PERFECT rep and builds your combo. Miss and you grind it out slowly and burn energy; run out and the rep fails. [ ] change weight · E leave · C camera.
