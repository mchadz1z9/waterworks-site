About us

A two-person business from Thornhill, not a franchise with a call centre.

Who we are

A small local business

WaterWorks Lawn & Landscaping is run by two people who live in the area. We started it to do outdoor work properly at a fair price.

Being small is the point. You deal with the same two people every time. There is no dispatcher and no rotating crew.

It also means we are honest about what we can take on. If a job is outside what we can do well, we say so rather than taking the money and guessing.

Where we work

Our service area

We take jobs anywhere within 15 km of us in Thornhill, Ontario.

Staying close is a deliberate choice. It keeps our prices where they are. It means we arrive on the day we said. It means we can come back quickly if something needs a second look.

If you are near the edge of that range, ask anyway. We will tell you honestly whether we can reach you rather than booking a job we cannot get to.

How we work

The rules we hold ourselves to

  • The price is said out loud before the work starts.
  • If a job turns out bigger than it looked, we stop and tell you.
  • We clean up after ourselves. Clippings off the path. Gates shut as we found them.
  • If we cannot make a day, you hear it from us first.
  • If something is not right, we come back and put it right.
  • We never charge for a visit that did not happen.

Our guarantee

If the work is not right, tell us within two days. We come back and fix it at no extra cost. That is the whole guarantee. There is no form to fill in and no argument about it.
