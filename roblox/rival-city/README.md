# Rival City — Roblox edition

`RivalCity.rbxlx` is a complete Roblox place: the city, the scripts, everything.

## Play it in Roblox Studio
1. Open **Roblox Studio** and sign in.
2. **File → Open from File…** and pick `RivalCity.rbxlx`.
3. Press **Play** (the ▶ button at the top) to test. Press **Stop** to go back to editing.

## Publish it (free)
1. **File → Publish to Roblox**, choose **New experience**, give it a name (e.g. *Rival City*), and click **Create**.
2. On the Roblox website, open **Create → your experience → Configure** and set it to **Public** when you're ready
   (Roblox may ask a short questionnaire about the game's content first).
3. To let the game save cash, cars and houses: in Studio, **Home → Game Settings → Security → Enable Studio Access to API Services** (for testing),
   saving works automatically once it's published.

## What's in it
- Your own Roblox avatar — no gym, no muscles, just you.
- A 5×5 city: downtown towers, shops, two parks, a plaza with a fountain (where you spawn), a beach with real water to swim in.
- Cars: press **E** at a car (or walk into its seat). W/S gas and brake, A/D steer, Space to hop out. Five cars, from the free City Hatch to the $9,000 Hypercar.
- Rival Motors car dealer, a delivery depot (jobs with a speed bonus), houses for sale ($5,000 — you respawn at home and get double payday).
- Gold coins around town, payday every 5 minutes, day and night with street lights, a 📱 phone (garage, map taxi, jobs, help).

## Changing it
The city is generated by `build.py` from the scripts in `src/`. Edit those and run `python3 build.py` to make a new `RivalCity.rbxlx`,
or just edit things directly in Studio.
