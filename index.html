Lawn mowing

April through October

A full cut at an even height across the whole property, with the edges trimmed along walkways, driveways, fences and garden beds. We clear the clippings off the hard surfaces before we leave, because a job is not finished while there is still grass on the driveway.

Cutting height matters more than most people expect. We leave the grass a little longer in the height of summer so the roots stay shaded and the lawn holds its colour through dry weeks, and we take it shorter in spring and autumn when growth is quicker.

  • Front, back and side lawns cut in one visit
  • Trimmed along fences, beds, walls and around trees
  • Clippings blown off the driveway, path and sidewalk
  • Bagged or mulched back into the lawn, your choice
  • Weekly, every two weeks, or a single one-off cut

Pressure washing

Spring through autumn

Dirt, algae and green build-up come off concrete, pavers, decking, fencing and siding. We work in overlapping sections and rinse as we go, so you get an even surface rather than clean stripes with dirty lines between them.

Pressure is matched to the surface. Concrete and pavers take the full pressure. Wood decking, painted fencing and vinyl siding are done at a lower setting with a wider fan, because forcing it would raise the grain or drive water behind the boards.

  • Driveways, walkways and patios
  • Decks, fences and garden walls
  • House siding and garage doors
  • Bins, garden furniture and steps
  • Detergent used only where the surface can take it, then fully rinsed

Car washing

All year, weather permitting

Washed by hand on your own driveway, so nothing spins against your paint the way it does in an automatic bay. We use two buckets, one with soap and one to rinse the mitt, which keeps the grit off the panels instead of dragging it around them.

We dry the car by hand at the end rather than leaving it to air dry, because water left sitting on paint is what leaves the spots and marks that people blame on the wash.

  • Full exterior hand wash, panel by panel
  • Wheels and tyres cleaned separately with their own bucket
  • Windows and mirrors done last
  • Dried by hand to avoid water marks
  • Done at your house, so there is nowhere to drive to

Snow removal

December through March

Driveways and walkways cleared early in the morning, so you are not out shovelling before work or school. We clear down to the surface rather than leaving a packed layer that turns to ice by the afternoon.

Where the snow goes matters as much as clearing it. We pile it where it will not block your view backing out of the driveway and where it will not slide straight back across the path when it starts to melt.

  • Driveway cleared end to end, down to the surface
  • Front walkway and steps
  • Path cleared around the car if it is parked outside
  • Snow piled clear of sightlines and drainage
  • Booked per snowfall, never as a season contract

Questions

Things people ask us

Which days can you actually come?

Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays. We are not available the rest of the week, so if you need a particular date it is worth emailing a week or so ahead.

Do I have to sign anything?

No. There is no contract and no minimum number of visits. Book one cut, or book every two weeks for the whole season, whichever suits you.

Do I need to be home?

Not for mowing, pressure washing or snow clearing, as long as we can reach the areas that need doing. For a car wash the car needs to be accessible in the driveway.

What happens if it rains?

We move you to the next working day and let you know. You are never charged for a visit that did not happen.

Is the starting price what I will actually pay?

For a standard property, yes. If the lot is unusually large or the job is heavier than it looked, we tell you the revised price before starting and you decide then.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Tell us and we will come back and sort it out. We would much rather fix it than leave it sitting there looking wrong.

Do you need to use my water and power?

For pressure washing and car washing, yes, an outdoor tap and an outdoor outlet. Mowing and snow clearing need neither.

How far ahead should I book?

A week is comfortable. We only have three working days, so the closer it gets the more likely those days are already taken.
