WaterWorks business card - files for the printer

Upload ONE of these:
  business-card-print.pdf   both sides in one file (page 1 = front, page 2 = back)
  card-front-print.pdf + card-back-print.pdf   if the printer asks for separate files

Order settings:
  Size: standard 3.5" x 2" (files are 3.75" x 2.25" including 1/8" bleed)
  Printed: both sides (double-sided), full colour
  Paper: 14pt or 16pt, matte or uncoated (easy to write on)
  Quantity: 2,000 (or the 2,500 pack if it costs about the same)

The PNG files are 300 dpi images of each side, for printers that only take images
or for posting online.
