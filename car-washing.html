Car washing

Available Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays. All year, weather permitting.

About this service

We wash the car by hand on your own driveway. Nothing spins against the paint the way it does in an automatic bay.

We use two buckets. One holds the soap and one rinses the mitt. That keeps grit off the panels instead of dragging it around them.

We dry the car by hand at the end. Water left sitting on paint is what causes the spots people blame on the wash.

How long it takes. A standard car takes roughly 30 to 45 minutes.

What you get

Everything on this list is part of the price. None of it is an extra.

Included

  • Full exterior hand wash, panel by panel
  • Wheels and tyres cleaned with their own bucket
  • Windows and mirrors done last
  • Dried by hand to avoid water marks
  • Done at your house, so there is nowhere to drive to

Not included

  • Interior vacuuming or cleaning
  • Waxing, polishing or paint correction
  • Engine bay cleaning
  • Scratch or dent repair
