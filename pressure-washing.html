Pressure washing

Available Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays. Spring through autumn.

About this service

Dirt, algae and green build-up come off concrete, pavers, decking, fencing and siding.

We work in overlapping sections and rinse as we go. That gives you an even surface instead of clean stripes with dirty lines between them.

Pressure is matched to the surface. Concrete and pavers take full pressure. Wood, painted fencing and vinyl siding are done at a lower setting with a wider fan. Forcing those would raise the grain or drive water behind the boards.

How long it takes. A sidewalk is usually under an hour. A full driveway takes longer and depends on how bad the staining is.
