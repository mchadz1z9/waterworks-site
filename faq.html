Questions we get asked

If your question is not here, send it through the quote form and we will answer it.

Which days can you come?

Lawn mowing, pressure washing and car washing run on Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays. Snow removal runs any day of the week.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere within 15 km of us in Thornhill. If you are near the edge of that, ask and we will tell you straight.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. You get a price with no obligation to book. If the number does not work for you, that is the end of it.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There is no contract and no minimum number of visits. Book one job or book every two weeks.

Do I need to be home?

Not for mowing, pressure washing or snow clearing. For a car wash the car needs to be accessible in the driveway.

How do I pay?

Square or cash, after the job is finished. There are no deposits and no account to set up.

What happens if it rains?

We move you to the next working day and let you know. You are never charged for a visit that did not happen.

Is the starting price what I will actually pay?

For a standard property, yes. If the job is bigger than it looked, we tell you the new price before starting.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Tell us within two days. We come back and fix it at no extra cost.

Do you need my water and power?

For pressure washing and car washing, yes. We need an outdoor tap and an outdoor outlet. Mowing and snow clearing need neither.

How far ahead should I book?

About a week is comfortable for lawn care, because we only have three working days for it. Snow removal can be arranged the night before.

Can I book the same service repeatedly?

Yes. Tick the repeating option on the quote form and tell us how often you want us. Most people choose every two weeks.

Want a price for your property?

Fill in the quote form and you will get a real number back. We usually reply the same day.
