Snow removal

Available any day of the week. December through March.

About this service

We clear driveways and walkways early in the morning. You are not out shovelling before work or school.

We clear down to the surface. Leaving a packed layer behind is what turns to ice by the afternoon.

Where the snow goes matters as much as clearing it. We pile it clear of your sightlines when backing out, and clear of the path when it starts to melt.

How long it takes. Most driveways take 20 to 40 minutes, depending on how much fell.

What you get

Everything on this list is part of the price. None of it is an extra.

Included

  • Driveway cleared end to end, down to the surface
  • Front walkway and steps
  • Path cleared around the car if it is parked outside
  • Snow piled clear of sightlines and drainage
  • Booked per snowfall, never as a season contract
  • Available any day, including weekdays

Not included

  • Salting or de-icing
  • Roof or gutter snow removal
  • Commercial lots
  • Clearing during an active storm
